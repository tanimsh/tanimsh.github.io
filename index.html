CONNECTING PLACE, SYSTEMS, & PEOPLE.

Risk & Decisions · Infrastructure Interdependencies · Emergency Responses

I am an Assistant Professor in Kansas State University's Department of Landscape Architecture and Regional & Community Planning and a Faculty Scholar at the Kansas Water Institute. I examine how environmental change and infrastructure performance affect communities, with a focus on water-infrastructure resilience, cascading system failures, disaster evacuation, transportation planning, and public health response. I help agencies and communities develop sustainable, resilient solutions.

Research

Research thrust

Decision-ready geospatial and AI research for building resilient community, integrating human behavior, watershed, infrastructure systems, environmental conditions, and public-health access to support real-world planning and emergency decisions.

01

Infrastructure & Water Systems Resilience

Flood, dams, utility networks, transportation, and other infrastructure interdependencies, and resilience across disaster phases.

02

Decision-Ready GIS & Human–Environment System

GeoAI, spatial decision-support tools, network modeling, and community-centered approaches to environmental challenges.

03

Risk Analysis & Behavior

Evacuation behavior, neighborhood-level risk, demand prediction, and equity-centered emergency planning.

04

Geospatial AI for Community Health

Mobile health clinic placement, rural health access, infectious-disease forecasting, and health-service utilization.
